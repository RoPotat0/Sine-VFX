Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This policy explains what information SineVFX collects when you connect your Roblox account through our Discord bot, and how we use it. By connecting your account you agree to this policy.

What we collect

When you choose to connect an account, we receive and store only:

  • Your Discord user ID (the numeric ID, so we know who to give the buyer role to).
  • Your Roblox user ID and username, obtained through Roblox's official OAuth login.
  • Whether that Roblox account owns the SineVFX plugin, and the time we last checked.

We request the openid, profile, and user.inventory-item:read scopes from Roblox: enough to confirm your identity and check whether you own the SineVFX plugin. We do not receive your password, email, or payment details, and we only look for ownership of this one plugin, not the rest of your inventory.

How we use it

  • To verify that you own SineVFX and grant the buyer role in our Discord server.
  • To re-check your status when you run the /update command.

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database and is accessible only to the SineVFX operator. It is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data removal

You can ask us to delete your linked account data at any time. Contact us in the SineVFX Discord server and we'll remove your records. Removing the connection also removes the buyer role.

Third-party services

The connect flow relies on Roblox (OAuth login and ownership check), Discord (roles and messaging), and Cloudflare (hosting and database). Your use of those services is also governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the Discord server.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us in the SineVFX Discord server.
